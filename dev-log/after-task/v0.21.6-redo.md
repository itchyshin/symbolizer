## What shipped

The `symbolizer-gllvm.Rmd` article rebuilt around the Nakagawa et al.
(in prep) syndromes + integrated plasticity framework:

- §1 names both tiers up front (syndromes + integrated plasticity).
- §3 NEW univariate motivation (lme4 random intercept; classical
  repeatability $R = \sigma^2_u / (\sigma^2_u + \sigma^2_e)$).
- §4 NEW factor-analytic motivation (parameter-counting argument).
- §6 Widget 1 — Behavioural syndromes (Σ_B only).
- §7 Widget 2 — Adds integrated plasticity (Σ_W). σ_ε auto-suppression
  blockquote with both bullets from gllvmTMB's actual message.
- §10 NEW — the glmmTMB bridge (latent()/unique() ↔ rr()/diag()).
- §11 capability list updated for v0.21.6-redo.

Extractor `symbolize.gllvmTMB` populates the two-tier widget-shape
slots on `sym$expanded`:

- `Lambda_W`, `Z_W`, `Psi_W`, `Sigma_W`, `Repeatability` (Tasks 3-6).
- `Sigma_B` now uses the algebraic closure
  $\Lambda_B \Lambda_B^\top + \mathrm{diag}(\Psi_B^2)$ rather than
  `fit$report$Sigma_B` (which omits the Psi_B contribution).

Renderer (`as_html_three_views`) Tab 3 emits a per-tier implied-cov
block via new `latex_implied_cov_block()` plus a per-trait R_t row
when both tiers are present.

`glm_build_distribution()` AND `glm_build_components()` extended with
a `has_within` flag so Widget 2's Tabs 1 and 2 (panel-idx, panel-eq)
render the two-tier equations — fix for the V2/V3 blocker.

CSV updates: `capabilities.csv` adds Sigma_W + Repeatability rows;
`assumption-templates.csv` adds the Sigma_W covariance_decomposition
row; `family-parameterizations.csv` description mentions the
two-tier story and R_t readout.
