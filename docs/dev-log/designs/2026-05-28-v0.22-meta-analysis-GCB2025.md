The earlier design (this morning) scoped three independent flavors with three independent widgets and no combined-model widget. The maintainer's overnight ask escalated:

> "I think you need to go up to using a drmTMB or brms, or I think my preferred drmTMB to do all the way up to location-scale meta-analysis. Look at my Global Change Biology paper. I mean, we can go all the way up to phylogenetic, double hierarchical or location-scale meta-analysis."

The GCB paper Nakagawa et al. 2025 (*Global Change Biology* 31(5), e70204; [DOI 10.1111/gcb.70204](https://doi.org/10.1111/gcb.70204)) — "Location-Scale Meta-Analysis and Meta-Regression as a Tool to Capture Large-Scale Changes in Biological and Methodological Heterogeneity" — is the anchor. Its **Bonus 2** model (location-scale phylogenetic meta-regression) fits all three flavors at once. The article's pedagogical goal is to **build up** to that one model, not present three disconnected ones.

## 2. The anchor model (Bonus 2 from Nakagawa et al. 2025)

In `brms` syntax (the paper's reference implementation):

```r
brms::brm(
  bf(
    es | se(sqrt(vi)) ~ 1 + habitat
        + (1 | species_ID)
        + (1 | study_ID)
        + (1 | gr(es_ID, cov = vcv))         # observation-level known V
        + (1 | gr(phylogeny, cov = mat)),    # phylogenetic A
    sigma ~ 1 + habitat
  ),
  data  = thermal,
  data2 = list(mat = A_phylo, vcv = V_obs),
  family = gaussian()
)
```

**Structurally**: five random-effect tiers plus a fixed effect on the mean plus a fixed effect on `sigma`. The marginal variance for effect $kt$ in study $k$ is

$$
\mathrm{Var}(y_{kt}) = v_{kt} + \tau^2_{kt} + \sigma_p^2 A_{kk} + \sigma_s^2 + \sigma_{\text{study},k}^2 + \sigma_{\text{es},kt}^2,
$$

with $\log \tau_{kt} = \gamma_0 + \gamma_1 \cdot \text{habitat}_{kt}$ (the location-scale piece). The paper's headline finding: aquatic organisms show *both* larger mean thermal-acclimation responses *and* higher between-study heterogeneity than terrestrial ones — patterns invisible to homoscedastic meta-regression.

**Package limitation noted in the paper**: `metafor` (`rma.mv`) cannot fit a `sigma ~ moderator` submodel; `blsmeta` is limited. Only `brms` (the paper) — and, we propose, `drmTMB` (this article) — can fit the full Bonus 2 model. **P1 verifies drmTMB's coverage; see §11.**

## 4. Widget anatomy (per the gllvm Σ-decomposition template)

Each widget has three tabs (Index / Matrix / Equations-with-data) and follows the gllvm article's pattern: Tab 3 emits a numerical implied-covariance block. The covariance composition grows widget by widget.

**Widget 1 Tab 3** — single Σ-block for the marginal variance:

$$
\mathrm{Var}(y_k) = v_k + \tau^2.
$$

**Widget 2 Tab 3** — four-tier decomposition:

$$
\mathrm{Var}(y_{kt}) = v_{kt} + \sigma_{\text{es}}^2 + \sigma_{\text{study}}^2 + \sigma_s^2 + \sigma_p^2 A_{kk}.
$$

Per-tier blocks emit numerical matrices: $\sigma_p^2 \mathbf{A}$ (T × T phylo cov), $\sigma_s^2 \mathbf{I}_S$, $\sigma_{\text{study}}^2 \mathbf{I}_K$, $\sigma_{\text{es}}^2 \mathbf{I}_n$. Plus a derived **per-trait repeatability**-analogue $R_t$ for each species, mirroring the gllvm Tab 3 R_t row.

**Widget 3 Tab 3** — adds the location-scale piece:

$$
\mathrm{Var}(y_{kt}) = v_{kt} + \tau^2(x_{kt}) + (\text{multilevel + phylo as above}),
\qquad \log \tau(x_{kt}) = \gamma_0 + \gamma_1 x_{kt}.
$$

Emits a small numerical table of $\hat \gamma_0, \hat \gamma_1$ and the implied $\hat \tau(x_{kt})$ for each level of `habitat`. Plus the **gain-over-Widget-2** diagnostic: model-comparison row (AIC for drmTMB / LOOIC for brms).

## 5. Per-package coverage (P1-conditional)

| Package | Widget 1 (traditional) | Widget 2 (phylo + multilevel) | Widget 3 (+ location-scale) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `metafor` | **deep** (`rma(yi, vi)`) | light (`rma.mv(yi, V=vi, random = ~1\|species, R = list(species=A))`) | **not supported** by `metafor` per Nakagawa et al. 2025 — explicit "not in scope this package" note |
| `drmTMB` | light (`drmTMB(yi ~ 1, weights = 1/vi)`) | **deep** (full model via `phylo()` + `meta_V()` + multiple random intercepts) | **deep** (Bonus-2: + `sigma ~ habitat`) — P1 confirmed |
| `brms` | light (`brm(yi \| se(sqrt(vi)) ~ 1 + (1\|study))`) | light | **light** (side-by-side equivalence Face — same math, different idiom) |
| `glmmTMB` | light (`glmmTMB(yi ~ 1 + (1\|study), weights = 1/vi, dispformula = ~0)`) | not supported beyond traditional (`equalto` / `propto` constraints; see existing `symbolizer-meta.Rmd`) | not supported |
| `MCMCglmm` | light (`MCMCglmm(yi ~ 1, random = ~ study, mev = vi)`) | light (`ginverse = list(species = Ainv)`) | not directly supported (no scale-modelling) — note as a limitation |

**Lead-package selection is P1-conditional:**
- **If P1 says drmTMB fits the full Bonus 2 model**: drmTMB leads §4 + §5; brms is the side-by-side equivalence Face.
- **If P1 says drmTMB falls short**: brms leads §5; drmTMB shows the partial model + a "for the full model, use brms" callout.

Either way, both packages render their fitted Σ-tiers via the symbolizer extractor.

## 6. Pedagogical principles (mirrored from gllvm article)

- **Named concept before symbol.** "Traditional pooling" → $\tau^2$. "Phylogenetic heterogeneity" → $\sigma_p^2 \mathbf{A}$. "Integrated plasticity" — wait, that was gllvm. For meta-analysis: "moderator-driven heterogeneity" → $\gamma_1$.
- **One dataset, watch numbers migrate.** thermal.csv used in §3 → §4 → §5; readers see $\tau^2$ split into the four random-effect contributions, then split again into baseline-vs-modulator scale.
- **Single anchor equation reused everywhere.** $\mathrm{Var}(y_{kt}) = v_{kt} + \text{[structured pieces]}$ appears in every widget's Tab 1 and Tab 2.
- **Defaults made explicit (lavaan / gllvmTMB pattern).**
  - metafor's REML default.
  - drmTMB's default link on `sigma` (`log` for Gaussian).
  - brms's prior choices for $\sigma_p, \sigma_s, \gamma_1$ (LKJ on correlations; horseshoe option for moderators).
  - The `offset(0.5*log(vi))` trick in the σ-submodel — what it does, why it's needed.
- **Same widget anatomy across all three.** Tab 1 indexed, Tab 2 matrix, Tab 3 worked-example + implied-covariance decomposition block.

## 10. Out of scope (explicit non-goals)

- **Multivariate meta-analysis** (rma.mv with struct = "UN"). Already covered for metafor in the existing `symbolizer-meta.Rmd` (now renamed to `symbolizer-meta-advanced.Rmd` per the prior design's recommend).
- **Spatial meta-analysis** (Mizuno et al. 2026 unified phylo + spatial). Separate future article.
- **Publication-bias / selection models** (Egger, PET-PEESE, RoBMA, copas). Out of symbolizer's symbolic surface entirely.
- **Network meta-analysis** (indirect comparisons). Different model class; future article if requested.
- **MCMCglmm location-scale** — MCMCglmm doesn't natively expose a scale submodel; the article notes it as a limitation rather than implementing a workaround.

## 11. Slice prerequisites — verification items

- **P1 — drmTMB capability check** (resolved 2026-05-28): drmTMB **PASSES** via package-specific idioms. Verification report: `docs/dev-log/figure-audits/v0.22-drmtmb-capability/capability-report.md`. Key findings:
  - Known sampling variance: drmTMB's `meta_V(V = vi)` replaces brms's `se(sqrt(vi))`.
  - Observation-level vcv: `meta_V(V = V_matrix)` replaces `(1 | gr(es_ID, cov = vcv))`.
  - Phylogenetic random intercept: `phylo(1 | phylogeny, tree = tree)` replaces `(1 | gr(phylogeny, cov = A))`.
  - Scale submodel: `drm_formula(..., sigma ~ 1 + habitat)` works.
  - `offset()` in σ formula is NOT supported but is REDUNDANT in drmTMB because `meta_V` already pins the sampling-variance scale at the mean-equation level. **This is a teachable cross-package design difference**, not a limitation.
  - Convergence: clean on the 250-effect / 35-species subsample (logLik = -11.61, no gradient warnings).
  - **Verdict**: drmTMB leads §5 (location-scale); brms is the side-by-side equivalence Face. The article teaches both idioms as alternative syntaxes for the same math.
- **P2 — brms fit round-trips**: confirm `brms::brm(...)` with the full Bonus 2 spec converges on the chosen subsample and the symbolizer extractor surfaces the sigma submodel correctly.
- **P3 — thermal.csv subsample reproducibility**: confirm a small (~200-effect) deterministic subsample preserves the habitat aquatic-vs-terrestrial scale-difference finding qualitatively. If the small subset washes the signal out, the article uses the full dataset with cached brms fits.
